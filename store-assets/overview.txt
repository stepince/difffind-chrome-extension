DiffFind Launcher

Find what changed. Understand what matters.

Open DiffFind in its own dedicated window straight from the Chrome toolbar. Launch again and you return to the same window, with your current comparison exactly as you left it. Close the window and the next launch opens a fresh one.

Features
• One click from the toolbar opens DiffFind in a focused, app-style window
• Re-launching restores and focuses your existing window instead of opening duplicates
• Self-hosting friendly: set your own DiffFind address (for example https://difffind.example.com) in the Configure page, or reset to the default
• No accounts, no tracking, no extra permissions

How to use
1. Click the DiffFind icon in the Chrome toolbar, then Open DiffFind.
2. Self-hosting? Click Configure in the popup, enter your DiffFind URL, and Save.

About this extension
This is a lightweight launcher. Comparisons run in the DiffFind web application, either the hosted version at https://app.difffind.com or your own instance, and require a connection to that site. The extension does not read page content, browsing history, documents, or AI-provider keys.

Permission
storage: remembers the ID of your DiffFind window for the current browser session and the site URL you configure. Nothing is sent anywhere by the extension.
